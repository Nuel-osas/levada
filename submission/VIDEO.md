# Levada: 2-minute pitch video

About 280 spoken words at a calm pace. Record the screen with a voice-over; no camera needed.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page hero, then deck slide 2 | "There's about a million MUSD sitting on Base, and right now it earns almost nothing. The best yield for MUSD, Mezo's own savings rate, only exists on Mezo. Getting there means bridging by hand, buying BTC for gas, and doing it all again to get out." |

| 1:25–1:40 | Deck slide 5 | "Levada doesn't compete with Mezo. It extends sMUSD to every MUSD holder on Base, it turns idle MUSD into sMUSD demand, and lsMUSD is a standard ERC-4626 token that other Base protocols can build on." |
